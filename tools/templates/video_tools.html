What Are AI Video Tools?

AI video tools use artificial intelligence to generate, animate, edit or transform video content. Different platforms can support text-to-video generation, image animation, digital avatars, video editing and other creative production workflows.

AI Video Generators

AI video generators can create scenes and moving visual content from prompts, images or other source material. These tools may be useful for social media, creative concepts, marketing, filmmaking experiments and visual storytelling.

Image-to-Video and Animation

Image-to-video platforms can add motion to photographs, illustrations and AI-generated images. Different platforms offer different levels of motion control, duration, resolution and creative effects.

AI Avatar Video Tools

AI avatar platforms can produce presenter-style content for explainers, training, marketing, product demonstrations and business communication without requiring a traditional filming setup.
