Three different video problems

Generate a new shot

Flow, Runway, Pika and Luma are relevant when the core need is synthetic motion or a shot that does not exist yet.

Edit and finish

An editor such as CapCut is better suited to pacing, captions, audio, effects and final short-form assembly.

Deliver a script with a presenter

HeyGen, D-ID and Synthesia focus on digital presenters for explainers, training, marketing and business communication.
